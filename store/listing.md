# Play Store listing copy

Drafts for the Play Console listing. Written from the site's own words —
`home.lead`, `home.fc.heading` and `about.missionBody` — so the listing
and the site do not drift into describing two different products.

Play strips formatting from the description. Everything below is plain
text with line breaks; the capitalised headings are how Play listings
conventionally fake structure.

Character limits: title 30, short description 80, full description 4000.

## Review notes for Google — do not skip this

The app is **invite-gated**, and Google's reviewers will not be able to
get past the signup wall on their own. An app a reviewer cannot exercise
is a standard rejection.

In Play Console → App content → **App access**, choose *All or some
functionality is restricted* and give them:

- a working email and password for a test account that is already a
  member of a church with some events on it
- a second account with church-owner access, if you want the dashboard
  reviewed rather than guessed at
- one line saying browsing churches and events needs no account, and
  everything else needs an invitation

Keep those credentials working. A reviewer hitting a dead login on a
later release is a rejection on a version that was otherwise fine.

## Data safety — what to declare

The app collects, at minimum: name, email address, approximate location,
and payment information (handled by Stripe, not stored by FaithDock).
Giving and event ticketing are financial transactions and must be
declared as such. The site's privacy policy is the reference, and the
form must agree with it — a mismatch between the two is something Google
does check.
